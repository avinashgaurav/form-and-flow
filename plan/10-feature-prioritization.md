# 10 · Feature prioritisation

Scored with a simple, explicit method so you can argue with any row. All scores are **[REC]** judgements.

## 1. Method

- **Value** (1 to 5): how much it moves the north star (file 17) or a top job (file 05).
- **Risk reduction** (1 to 5): how much it lowers safety, trust or legal risk.
- **Effort** (1 to 5): build plus content plus review, for a solo founder with AI coding tools.
- **Score** = (Value + Risk reduction) / Effort. Ties broken by "does the pilot loop break without it?"

Must-haves for safety or law are P0 regardless of score.

## 3. What the scoring says

1. The cheapest high-value items are behavioural (check-in, shorten, one next action, memory viewer). Build them first.
2. The **food catalogue** is the riskiest P0: high value, high effort, and blocked on data licensing (E-05, E-07, T-13). Start licensing conversations in week 1 (file 19).
3. **Coach chat** is the most expensive P0. If the build runs late, ship the pilot with the weekly review and action cards first, and the open chat second; F-16 is then re-scoped to action cards for gate G-07, and the change is recorded.
