# 26 · Filming guide: real clips to replace the 3D demos

Every exercise now has a 3D demo. Real clips replace them one by one for beta (plan 09: technique media shows real people, checked by a coach). You film; I trim, loop, crop and compress.

## Setup (once)

- **Phone:** on a tripod or shelf at hip height, about 3 m away, 1080p or 4K, landscape.
- **Place:** a plain light wall, even daylight, no mirrors behind you.
- **You:** fitted plain clothes (no logos), shoes on, nothing else in frame.
- **Other people:** if someone else performs, they sign a model release first. No other gym members in shot, no gym branding.

## Each clip

1. Stand side-on at a slight angle to the camera (about 45 degrees), like the 3D demos.
2. Start still, do **3 clean reps** at the app's tempo (about 1.5 s down, 1 s up), end still in the start position.
3. One clip per exercise. Name it with the app's ID, e.g. `goblet_squat.mov`, and drop it in `media-src/own-clips/`.

## After you film

I cut each clip to a seamless loop, crop it square, compress it under 1.5 MB, and swap it in for the 3D demo. A coach then signs off each clip before beta. Your footage is yours; nothing else needs a licence.
