# 01 · Executive summary

**Form & Flow (working name).** India-first AI fitness and nutrition coach for adults. Plan v0.3, 19 October 2026. Planning only; nothing is built.

**Product.** One weekly plan for training and food that adapts from what you log and explains every change. The coach talks; tested code decides. **The coach never states a number or safety decision that code did not compute** (Kokum's rule, reused).

**Gap [FACT, claims M-04, M-06, M-09 to M-11, M-13].** Healthify, cult.fit and FITTR already combine training, food and coaching in India, through human coaches or an AI with an unpublished method. MacroFactor adapts both, but in two separate apps with no shared coaching; Fitbod adapts only training. **Wedge [HYP]:** automatic, explained adaptation of both, on Indian food, at app prices. India anchors: ₹999 to ₹6,300 a year; we test ₹1,499 to ₹2,999.

**MVP.** A personal pilot with you as user one: 24 features with pass/fail criteria; 60 to 80 exercises, about 300 Indian foods, about 50 reviewed evidence cards. Offline logging. Weight and calorie numbers optional.

**Safety.** Readiness screen, red-flag stop with emergency guidance, pain flow, eating-disorder-aware mode, no supplement advice, restricted modes for higher-risk groups. A dietitian and an S&C professional sign off every rule before anyone but you uses it.

**Stack [REC, file 11].** Expo SDK 58, Supabase in Mumbai, Claude behind an adapter. Model cost about $0.45 per user a month [FACT prices, HYP usage, T-23].

**Timeline [HYP].** Part-time: about 19 weeks from approval to pilot, 33 to the end of a 12 to 25 person beta.

**Cost to end of beta [HYP].** ₹3.5 to ₹16.2 lakh self-built with paid reviewers, legal and video; ₹16 to ₹41 lakh with hired developers. Infrastructure $26 to $99 a month.

**Top risks.** India's official food tables and 2024 dietary guidelines need NIN's written permission (fallback exists); reviewer availability; India's data law (DPDP) taking full effect on 13 May 2027, mid-beta; Healthify or MacroFactor closing the gap first.

**Kokum.** A later extension of this app; not in the current build (file 24). **Pricing:** free for now; decided later.

**What I need from you.** Approve the plan, then the open decisions in file 23 §2: budget, colour and skin, individual or company, name, chat retention, language, food-data fallback, exercise media, AI data retention. Professionals decide rule values; the product team decides implementation.
