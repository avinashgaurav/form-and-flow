# 20 · Launch plan

Four stages. Each stage has an entry gate, a size, a stop rule and an exit decision. **[REC]** throughout.

## Stage A · Founder pilot (n = 1, 4+ weeks)

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| Entry gate | Pilot release gate (file 16 §4) |
| Distribution | Development or internal builds on your own phones (Android internal testing track; TestFlight internal testing on iOS) |
| Rhythm | Use daily; weekly scorecard (file 17 §6); fix Critical bugs within 48 h |
| Data | Real data in `prod` with full safeguards; export and delete your own account once as a test, then restore from export |
| Stop rule | Any unsafe output shown to you: stop, fix, re-run the eval suite |
| Exit decision | Go to beta prep, fix and extend the pilot, or stop. What the pilot **cannot** show: effectiveness or safety for anyone else |

## Stage B · Controlled beta (12 to 25 adults, about 6 weeks)

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| Entry gate | Beta release gate (file 16 §4); professional review of rules, content and templates; counsel review of notice and terms |
| Who | Adults across personas U1 to U4, at least 40 per cent vegetarian, mixed experience; same exclusions as the MVP (file 05) |
| Consent | Plain participation sheet: what the app is and is not, risks, data use, how to leave and delete; separate from the in-app consent |
| Distribution | Google Play closed testing and TestFlight external testing (Beta App Review applies). If you publish from a new personal Play developer account, Google requires a closed test with a minimum number of opted-in testers for a minimum number of days before production access (claim R-21); plan the beta to satisfy it |
| Support | In-app reports; a weekly 10-minute call with a rotating subset |
| Safety | All safety events reviewed within the targets in file 13 §6; weekly safety review with a reviewer |
| Stop rules | Any SEV1: pause new sign-ins, fix, notify affected testers. Two SEV2 in one week on the same rule: disable the rule by flag |
| Measures | File 17 metric tree and guardrails; price survey (H-10) |
| Exit decision | Launch, extend beta, or pivot scope |

## Stage C · Public India launch

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| Entry gate | Launch go/no-go (file 23) signed |
| Store listings | No medical or outcome claims (file 14 §7); Play description states the app is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat (R-12); privacy policy in-app and on the listing; Google Play Data safety and health apps declaration; Apple privacy details; in-app account deletion (claims R-12, R-15, R-17) |
| Pricing | Free tier plus Premium at the price validated in beta (file 06 §10, file 21) |
| Rollout | Play staged rollout and App Store phased release; feature flags for coach, weekly review and each rule family; kill switch per rule |
| Channels [HYP] | Fitness and vegetarian-cooking communities, honest creator partnerships disclosed per advertising rules (claim R-08), content about Indian food and training; no paid acquisition until week-4 retention is known |
| Support | Help centre; grievance contact; response targets |
| Monitoring | Daily guardrail review for the first 2 weeks |

## Stage D · After launch

| Track | When | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Possible Kokum bundle (separate product) | Your call | File 24 |
| Regional-language UI | When search logs (H-14) and retention justify it | Start with Hindi |
| New countries | Only after country-specific legal, store, food-data and content review | Do not just translate |

## Decision you need to make before Stage B

**Publish as an individual or as a company [OPEN].** It affects liability, how the developer name appears in stores, Apple organisation enrolment, and which Google Play testing requirements apply (R-21). Counsel and your accountant should advise; file 22 lists it.
